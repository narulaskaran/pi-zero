# Provisional landmark studies

Friday, Saturday and Sunday are provisional line illustrations selected for use while further aesthetic refinement is pending. They are not historic engravings or measured architectural/geographic records. Monday–Thursday remain unchanged.

- **Eiffel Tower:** newly authored editable vector, dedicated CC0 1.0. Structural facts referenced from https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/the-monument/history (not an image source). License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Raster matches the selected threshold-190 proof after fitting to the art region.
- **Space Needle:** newly authored adaptation after https://freesvg.org/1553181540 (FreeSVG/OpenClipart, CC0). Added deck glazing, rim, supports and hatching are an interpreted study, not original source linework or verified engineering detail. Grayscale raster fits to the region, then uses Pillow 1-bit dithering to retain the selected proof appearance.
- **Mount Rainier:** newly authored interpretation informed by the Gustav Wilhelm Krollmann poster https://www.loc.gov/item/2010651155/ . LOC rights advisory: no known restrictions on publication, published without copyright notice; users remain responsible for assessing rights. Internal terrain contours are illustrative, not verified glacier geography. Full-resolution raster, tight crop and white margin preserve the selected threshold-170 proof; this is not Krollmann's original linework.

Editable SVGs are bundled alongside their raster derivatives. Catalog SHA-256 values identify the exact raster inputs. Source fitting is performed offline; ordinary display requests do not download reference images. Existing fonts remain under their bundled OFL licenses.
